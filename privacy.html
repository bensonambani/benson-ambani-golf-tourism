Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This page explains, in general terms, how Benson Ambani Golf & Tourism may handle information you choose to share when you visit this website or contact us.

Information you provide

If you submit a booking, advertising or feedback enquiry, the information you enter may be transferred to WhatsApp when you choose to send the prepared message. This can include your name, contact details, preferred service or date, and the message you provide. You decide whether to send it.

How information is used

Information voluntarily sent to us may be used to respond to enquiries, discuss availability and service arrangements, provide requested information, and manage customer communications.

Third-party services

This website links to third-party services including WhatsApp and social media platforms. Their own privacy policies and terms apply when you use those services. We do not control their data practices.

Cookies and advertising

This website may not set its own analytics or advertising cookies at present. If analytics, embedded content or advertising services are added later, this policy should be updated to explain the providers, purposes and available choices before those services are enabled.

Data security and retention

Reasonable care should be taken when handling enquiries. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or other sensitive information through the website forms. Enquiry messages may be retained for as long as reasonably needed to respond and maintain relevant business records, subject to applicable law.

Your choices and questions

For questions about information you have sent us, contact bensonambani97@gmail.com or +254 768 971 515.

This is a general website privacy notice, not legal advice. The site owner should review it against actual data practices and applicable Kenyan data-protection requirements before relying on it.
