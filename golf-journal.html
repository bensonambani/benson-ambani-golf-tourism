Practical notes for new golfers, equipment buyers and people planning a golf experience in Kenya.

Getting Started with Golf in Kenya

Golf can feel complicated when you are new to it, but you do not need to buy a full set of clubs immediately. Start by learning the basic rules, etiquette, grip, stance and putting. If possible, try beginner sessions or borrow suitable equipment before making a large purchase.

Before visiting a course, check its visitor access, dress code, booking requirements, practice facilities and applicable fees. A little preparation makes the first visit more comfortable.

Choose a learning pace that suits you and ask questions. Golf is a skill developed over time, and regular practice is more useful than rushing to buy expensive equipment.

What to Check When Buying Second-hand Golf Clubs

Inspect club heads for cracks, dents and unusual wear. Check the grooves on irons and wedges, the face and crown of woods, and the condition of grips. Look along each shaft for bends, corrosion or damage and check that the club feels secure.

Try to confirm the model, shaft type, flex and set composition. Make sure the clubs suit your height, strength, experience and budget. Ask the seller about the clubs' history and compare the asking price with similar equipment.

Photos can help with an initial review, but an in-person inspection is better where practical. If you are unsure, seek advice from a knowledgeable golfer or club fitter before paying.

Planning a Golf Day or Golf Visit

Start by choosing a course and checking visitor access, tee-time arrangements, dress code, equipment availability and current charges. Ask what is included in the quoted price and whether green fees, caddie fees, club hire, transport or meals are separate.

Allow enough time to arrive, check in and warm up. Bring comfortable golf clothing, suitable shoes, water and any personal equipment you need. Respect course etiquette, staff instructions and pace-of-play guidance.

For a golf tourism visit, confirm the itinerary and costs with each provider before travelling. Conditions and availability can change, so reconfirm important arrangements close to the date.
